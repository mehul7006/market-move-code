Segments — click any card for its individual signal

Educational research tool — not investment advice. Every number on this desk comes from the live Upstox feed (or Yahoo real candles as a labelled fallback) — there is no mock mode. If a feed fails you will see a red error, never invented data. Calls are issued only for Sensex, Nifty, Bank Nifty, Fin Nifty, Midcap Select and Next 50; sector indices are chart context only. Each segment runs its own six-month tested profile (session-bounded at 15:25 IST, unanimous indicators, wide stop) and may call only while its measured T1-before-stop rate is ≥75%. Those are in-sample base rates, not a forward guarantee: the wide stop puts R:R near 0.25–0.4, so break-even is roughly 71–80% before costs. Derivatives carry substantial risk of loss.

Today's calls — six index segments, replayed through the live gates

Every setup today's own rules would have issued on Sensex, Nifty, Bank Nifty, Fin Nifty, Midcap Select and Next 50, with entry, stop, T1/T2/T3, result, duration and stop-hit count. Outcomes close at 15:25 IST: a timeout is not a win. No lookahead.

Next-day model — buy today's close, sell tomorrow's close

A separate long-only overnight book. It only speaks when an index closes oversold (RSI 14 ≤ 38 and a 3-day return ≤ −1%), buys that close, and exits at the next session's close. The intraday desk above is unchanged and keeps its own 75% profiles.
